Low-Resource Language Chatbot

  • Tech Stack: Python, mBERT, Transformers, PyTorch, Transfer Learning
  • Research Area: Conversational AI, NLP

Developed a conversational AI chatbot for low-resource languages using fine-tuned multilingual BERT and transfer learning techniques.

The chatbot was designed to improve accessibility for speakers of underrepresented languages, with an emphasis on Amharic and Tigrinya.

The model achieved 85.7% intent recognition accuracy and an 88.2% BLEU score while maintaining an average response latency of 1.9 seconds.

This work demonstrates how modern transformer models can improve linguistic inclusion by enabling intelligent conversational systems for communities that are often underserved by existing AI technologies.

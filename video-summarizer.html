Multi-Lingual Video Summarizer using Llama 3

  • Tech Stack: Python, Llama 3, LangChain, Ollama, NLP
  • Research Area: Natural Language Processing, Generative AI

Developed a multilingual YouTube video summarization system powered by Llama 3 that generates concise, language-aware summaries from long-form video transcripts.

Designed an end-to-end pipeline for transcript processing, language-specific summarization, and multilingual output generation, allowing users to consume educational content more efficiently.

The system achieved 91.2% BLEU and 88.5% ROUGE-L, demonstrating strong translation quality and context preservation while maintaining a coherence rating of 4.8/5.

Optimized inference performance to summarize approximately 1,000 tokens in 3.5 seconds, providing scalable and responsive multilingual summarization.
